A colorful one-finger arcade game by Alen Brunec / AB DIGITECH.

How to play

The screen scrolls upward on its own and a white light trail follows your finger. Touch anywhere and drag to steer; the run starts with your first touch. Dodge green spiked blocks, blue spiked balls and spinning spiked bars, some of which move along paths. Touching one ends the run. Your score is the distance travelled, and the further you go, the faster it gets. Your best and last score are stored on your device. Use the speaker button to toggle sound.

Need help?

Contact support@abdigitech.net. Please include your device model, iOS or Android version and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords or payment details.

No account is required to play. The game shows Google AdMob advertising between attempts and privacy choices where required. See the privacy policy for advertising data and consent details.
